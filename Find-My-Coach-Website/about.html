What is Find My Coach?

Connect athletes with pro coaches nearby.

Find My Coach is a mobile app designed to help athletes discover professional sports coaches in their area across tennis, swimming, badminton, and dozens of other disciplines. Compare experience, training styles, and rates, then book sessions instantly with built-in messaging to stay aligned.

For coaching businesses

Grow, showcase, and manage in one place.

Coaches use Find My Coach to create polished profiles, reach more local athletes, and streamline all of their scheduling. The platform replaces ad-hoc marketing with a steady pipeline of motivated athletes so coaches can focus on helping people improve and succeed.

For Athletes

Find coaches who match your goals.

  • Connect with local coaches in seconds.
  • Choose from a wide variety of sports and specialties.
  • Enjoy instant booking and in-app messaging.

For Coaches

Grow your client roster, effortlessly.

  • Communicate in-app with every athlete lead.
  • Tap into a greater customer database nearby.
  • Run schedule and booking management from one hub.
