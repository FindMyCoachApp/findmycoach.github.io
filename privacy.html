Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 01, 2026

Find My Coach ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.findmycoach.my and the Find My Coach mobile application. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Waitlist & contact details: name, email, phone, and whether you are an athlete or coach when you sign up or contact us.
  • Usage data: anonymised analytics about how visitors use our website (only when analytics is enabled and consented to).
  • App data: when the app launches, profile, booking, and messaging data as described in the full policy.

How we use your information

  • To notify you about launch updates and early access
  • To respond to enquiries and partnership requests
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations in Malaysia

Sharing & retention

We do not sell your personal data. We may use trusted service providers (e.g. form hosting, analytics) who process data on our behalf. We retain waitlist data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing findmycoach.contact@gmail.com.
