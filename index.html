About Us

Connecting Athletes with Coaches

Find My Coach is a Kuala Lumpur–based sports platform built to connect athletes with independent coaches through a simple, flexible mobile app. We help athletes find trusted local coaches with transparent reviews and easy booking, while giving coaches the visibility, control, and freedom to manage their coaching business without contracts or commission fees.

Our mission is to make quality coaching more accessible and to support the growth of local sports communities. Starting with tennis, badminton, and swimming, Find My Coach is launching soon and aims to become the go-to platform for coaching in Kuala Lumpur before expanding to new sports and locations.

FAQ

Everything you need to know about Find My Coach and how it works for both athletes and coaches.

Find My Coach is a mobile app that connects athletes with professional sports coaches in their area. Whether you're looking for tennis, swimming, badminton, or other sports coaching, you can browse profiles, compare rates, read reviews, and book sessions instantly.

We're targeting a launch in the middle of 2026. Join our waitlist to be notified as soon as we go live!

We support 10+ sports including tennis, swimming, badminton, basketball, soccer, volleyball, and more. We're constantly adding new sports based on demand!

Coaches can join our waitlist now! Once we launch, you'll be able to create a profile, showcase your expertise, set your rates, and start connecting with athletes in your area.

The app is free for athletes to download and browse coaches. Coaches pay a small monthly fee to have access to the app.

Coaches get a 3 free month trial to try out the app. Also, everyone that registers will be notified as soon as we launch.
