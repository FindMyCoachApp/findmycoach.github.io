Find My Coach launching towards the end of 2026 in Kuala Lumpur

1 July 2026 · Find My Coach team

We are building Find My Coach to make it easier for athletes in Kuala Lumpur to find trusted, independent sports coaches, and for coaches to grow their business on their own terms. After months of development and conversations with local athletes and coaches, we are now targeting a launch towards the end of 2026.

At launch, the app will cover Kuala Lumpur first, with sports including tennis, badminton, swimming, and padel. Athletes will be able to browse coach profiles, compare rates, read reviews, and book sessions. Coaches get a simple monthly membership with 0% commission on bookings.

Who is it for?

Athletes looking for private or group coaching across KL, from beginners to competitive players. Coaches who want to get discovered beyond word of mouth, manage bookings in one place, and keep every ringgit they earn.

The waitlist is open now. Join for launch updates and early access when we go live. Coaches on the waitlist get a 3 month free trial at launch.
