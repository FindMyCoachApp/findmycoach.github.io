Coach waitlist: 3 month free trial at launch

3 July 2026 · Find My Coach team

We want founding coaches in Kuala Lumpur to start strong. That is why coaches who join the waitlist before launch get a 3 month free trial when Find My Coach goes live towards the end of 2026.

What you get

  • 3 months free of coach membership at launch
  • 0% commission on every session you book through the app
  • RM 30/month flat fee after your trial ends
  • Launch notification when the app goes live in KL

How to sign up

Visit our coach waitlist page, fill in the form, and select Coach as your role. Waitlist signups are free. No payment until after your trial.

Questions? Read our For Coaches page or message us on WhatsApp.
