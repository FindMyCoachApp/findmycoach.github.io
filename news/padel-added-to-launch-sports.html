Padel joins our launch sports lineup in KL

5 July 2026 · Find My Coach team

Padel is one of the fastest growing sports in Kuala Lumpur, with new courts opening across the city and demand for coaching rising fast. Based on feedback from athletes and coaches on our waitlist, we are adding padel to our launch sports lineup.

Find My Coach will launch in Kuala Lumpur with four sports: tennis, badminton, swimming, and padel. Basketball, football, and more will follow based on community demand.

For athletes

When the app goes live, you will be able to search for padel coaches by location, compare rates, and book sessions through the app. Tell us you play padel when you join the waitlist.

For padel coaches

If you coach padel in KL, join the coach waitlist for early access and a 3 month free trial at launch. Set your own rates, keep 0% commission, and get discovered by athletes looking for coaching.
